Privacy policy

Post, last updated November 14, 2026

Post is a desktop email client used only by its author, Francisco Rangel, with his own Google accounts.

What Post accesses

With the Google account owner's permission (the gmail.modify scope), Post reads messages, changes labels, archives, moves to trash, and sends mail. It never deletes mail permanently.

Where the data goes

Everything stays on the owner's computer. Post talks directly to Google's Gmail API; there is no Post server, and no data is sent to, sold to, or shared with any third party. Sign-in tokens and a small list of snoozed messages are stored locally, readable only by the owner.

Use of Google user data

Post's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, and local data is removed with post logout.

Contact
